To test the interactive files, run:

node question1-interactive.js

Then you can:
- Choose options from the menu (1-7)
- Enter amounts when prompted
- View transaction history
- Test undo and other features

Example interaction:
1. Choose "2" for Deposit
2. Enter "500" as amount
3. Choose "1" for Withdrawal  
4. Enter "200" as amount
5. Choose "6" to view history
6. Choose "7" to exit
